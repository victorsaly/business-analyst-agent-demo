What it does

Plans the analysis, writes and runs read-only SQL or pandas, fixes its own errors, draws a chart, explains what drove the result, flags anomalies, and writes a one-page weekly briefing.

Why you can trust it

The database is read-only. Every answer shows the exact query, taken from the tool log. It says when the data can't answer, and never presents a forecast as fact.

On the tape
